# Known Limitations & Honest Gaps

1. **Physical Distance & Velocity:** Mirage and the uncalibrated LiteRT relative-depth slot (`NativeDepthProvider`, `isMetricCalibrated == false`) do not provide calibrated metric distance or true object velocity. Until a calibrated hardware/depth pack is validated, Openetra reports image-relative zones (`foreground`, `left`, `center`, `right`, `overhead`), normalized relative proximity scores internally, and box-expansion looming cues only.
2. **Mirage Language & OCR Scope vs Bundled Indic OCR:** Mirage natively supports 7 European languages and Latin OCR; all 7 target Indic scripts (Hindi, Marathi, Kannada, Tamil, Telugu, Malayalam, Bengali) are bundled and verified offline via ML Kit Devanagari `16.0.1` + SHA-256-verified Tesseract `4.9.0` (`tessdata_fast`).
3. **Offline IndicConformer & Dedicated Currency/Depth Weights:** `OfflineSpeechPlugin` (`sherpa-onnx 2.0.3` + bundled `silero_vad.int8.onnx`), `NativeDepthProvider` / `SpecialistVisionPlugin` LiteRT `1.4.2` (`loadCurrencyModel` / `loadDepthModel`) runtimes are active and verified on Android 15 ARM64, whereas large downloadable `IndicConformer` ASR weights (~180–190 MB/language) and trained `.tflite` currency banknote / monocular depth model weights remain optional/pending dataset training.
4. **Kannada OCR Model Choice:** PaddleOCR lacks a PP-OCRv5 Kannada model (`ka_PP-OCRv3_mobile_rec` only); SHA-256-verified `tesseract-ocr/tessdata_fast` (`kan.traineddata`, `3,608,331 B`) is bundled and verified (`17 ms`, exact Kannada output on `Medium_Phone_API_35`).
5. **Indian Currency Classifier & Fraud Disclaimer:** Banknote `.tflite` training weights are pending; active recognition fuses Mirage `form` mode, Mirage Latin OCR numeral cross-check, and mandatory non-counterfeit uncertainty disclaimers. Openetra never claims a note is genuine or counterfeit.
6. **Hardware Verification Scope:** Host verification ran on Apple Silicon CPU; full Android 15 ARM64 verification (including R8 `app-release.apk` cold launch, 7 Indic OCR models, Silero VAD, Barcode, Mirage `form`, 96-dim embeddings, and LiteRT `1.4.2` XNNPACK) ran on the `Medium_Phone_API_35` emulator. Physical entry-level, mid-range, and flagship Android device battery/thermal benchmarks and blind-user field trials remain pending physical device testing.
7. **Safety Scope:** Openetra is a visual aid and **never** a substitute for a white cane, guide dog, or O&M training.
